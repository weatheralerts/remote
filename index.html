Flexibility works better with a forecast

The best part of remote and hybrid work is choosing when to do things. A walk at 11 instead of 1, errands before the rain, a café session on the one sunny afternoon of the week. But flexibility only helps if you know what is coming, and few people check hourly forecasts between calls.

Weather Alerts is an Android app that makes the forecast come to you. Set your own conditions and thresholds, choose when to hear, and receive a notification when they match. The Weather Alerts official site explains how alerts can arrive immediately or up to seven days before the expected weather.

Make it part of your planning ritual

  1. Save home, office and favourite spots. Add your home, the office and maybe a coworking space or park.
  2. Create a handful of rules. Heavy rain, gusty days, high heat index, a UV limit and freezing mornings cover most needs.
  3. Check alerts when planning the week. With up to seven days of notice, Monday morning planning can already include the weather.

Digital nomads and workations

If you work from different places, saved locations and live location weather make every new base feel familiar. Route weather keeps an eye on rain, snow, wind, storms, visibility and temperature between stops. Official severe weather warnings from supported national meteorological services show up with agency attribution, so you are not relying on local news in a language you may not speak.

Hourly and daily forecasts with temperature, feels-like, precipitation probability, wind, humidity, visibility, UV and air quality give you the detail to plan both work and exploring.

Fewer interruptions, better timing

Remote workers already juggle chat, email and calendar pings. Custom notification controls help: Active Hours deliver routine weather alerts when you actually want them, Quiet Hours keep evenings and nights calm, and critical warnings still get through. Custom sounds make weather alerts easy to recognise during a call, and widgets show conditions and active alerts without switching apps.

Air quality and wellbeing

Days with poor air quality are good days for an indoor workout. AQI and PM2.5 thresholds let you know in advance, and UV and air quality maps show conditions across your area. Weather Alerts provides information only and is not a health service.

Outdoor workspaces

Some remote workers like to take calls from the garden, a balcony or a park bench. Wind, rain, UV and temperature alerts help you pick the right days for it, and cloud cover rules tell you whether laptop screens will be fighting bright sun. Save the park or terrace you like as a location, and it gets its own set of conditions.

Planning ahead for the week

Monday morning is a good moment to look at upcoming alerts across your saved places. If a stormy day is coming, you can move in-person meetings, plan a delivery for a dry day or book a coworking space nearer home. With alerts arriving as early as seven days before, the forecast becomes part of your calendar rather than an afterthought.

The daily forecast covers several days of temperatures, rain, snow, clouds and wind, and the weather front view shows when changes are expected, which helps explain why a sunny morning may turn into a wet afternoon.

Family logistics for home workers

Working from home often means being the person who does the school run or walks the dog. Rain, wind and heat alerts for school and home help you fit those tasks into the driest or coolest part of the day, without leaving a meeting to discover it is pouring.

A sample week

Monday: a rain alert for Wednesday afternoon prompts you to swap your office day to Tuesday. Wednesday: a dry-window notification after a morning of rain lets you schedule a walking one-to-one call. Friday: a heat index alert for the afternoon means an early start and an early finish.

Data and Premium

Your workday forecasts are delivered by Apple Weather (WeatherKit), which combines data from national and international meteorological organisations, so the same trusted source follows you from the home desk to the office and any city you work from on the road.

Weather Alerts is available on Google Play for Android. Premium includes push notifications for custom alerts, more alerts and locations, extended forecasts and an ad-free experience, with a seven-day free trial billed by Google. Explore the Weather Alerts features before you start.

Frequently asked questions

Can I get notified when the rain stops so I can take a walk?

Yes. Rain alerts can notify you when precipitation is expected to start or stop.

Can it help me pick which days to go to the office?

Set rain, wind or snow alerts for your commute and check them when planning the week.

Will weather notifications interrupt my meetings?

Active Hours and Quiet Hours control when routine alerts arrive; critical warnings can still come through.

Does it work when I change cities?

Live location weather updates forecasts as your location changes, and you can save each base.

Can I see air quality before an outdoor workout?

You can set AQI and PM2.5 thresholds and check the air quality maps.

Is there a heat alert for working from home?

Temperature and heat index thresholds can be set for your home location.

Does it show official weather warnings abroad?

Warnings come from supported national meteorological services, so availability depends on the country.
